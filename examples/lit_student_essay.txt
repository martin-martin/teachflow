Change is Hard and Maybe About Weather

The novel I read this week shows that change is tough and also the sky changes, which might be the real point. I beleive the author wanted us to feel that the characters were friends but the arguement shifts around a lot so its not totally clear. Sometimes the narrator just says things happen and I guess we are supposed to agree. There are probably quotes that prove this but I am mostly going from memory instead of checking the book.

In the middle chapters the main character just keeps walking and thinking. This shows growth, or at least moving, but it also feels like nothing is happening becuase the scenes are short. The teacher in the story maybe represents rules, or maybe just weather again, because rain shows up a few times. I didnt use exact lines because the text was long and the due date was close.

Overall the story defintely hints that change is hard and sometimes friends dont help much. The ending is sudden and I am not sure what the author intended, but probably something about the seasons because leaves fall. In conclusion, change happens and thats what it is.
