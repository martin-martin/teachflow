# High School Literary Response Rubric (20 points)

**Thesis & Focus (0-4)**  
4: Clear, arguable thesis in the opening paragraph that guides the response.  
3: Thesis is present but somewhat vague or placed late.  
2: Topic is hinted at but no controlling idea.  
1-0: No discernible thesis or focus.

**Evidence & Analysis (0-4)**  
4: At least two specific quotations or details from the text are integrated and interpreted.  
3: One quotation/detail is used with some explanation.  
2: References are general or dropped in without analysis.  
1-0: No text-based support.

**Organization & Coherence (0-4)**  
4: Introduction, well-sequenced body, and purposeful conclusion with smooth transitions.  
3: Basic structure with minor lapses in flow.  
2: Choppy progression; missing or weak transitions.  
1-0: Disorganized or incomplete.

**Style & Voice (0-4)**  
4: Academic voice with varied syntax and precise diction.  
3: Mostly appropriate tone; some repetitive or casual phrasing.  
2: Noticeably informal; limited sentence variety.  
1-0: Slang-heavy or unclear.

**Conventions (0-4)**  
4: Virtually error-free grammar, spelling, and punctuation.  
3: A few minor errors that do not impede meaning.  
2: Frequent errors that occasionally distract.  
1-0: Persistent errors that hinder understanding.

**Total: /20**
